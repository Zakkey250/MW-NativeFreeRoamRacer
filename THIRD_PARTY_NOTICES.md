The unmodified header and license are in `NFSMWNativeFreeRoamRacers/third_party/nlohmann`; the binary ZIP includes `Licenses/nlohmann-json-LICENSE.MIT`. Used for bounded update-metadata parsing. Its upstream license is not replaced by the project's source-use terms.

## Audio extractor

See [the extractor notices](NFSU2EncounterAudioExtractor/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt) and its `Licenses` directory for the unchanged Python/Tcl/Tk runtime, PyInstaller bootloader exception, OpenSSL, libffi and zlib notices.

The public extractor ZIP **does not include vgmstream or codec DLLs**. Users obtain the unmodified Windows CLI bundle directly from [official vgmstream releases](https://github.com/vgmstream/vgmstream/releases) and keep its licenses. The decoder bundle used during earlier Japanese extraction testing reported `r2117-284-ge6afeaac`; no guarantee is made for every future decoder build.

抽出アプリ配布ZIPにvgmstream・コーデックDLLは同梱しません。利用者が公式配布物を別途取得し、その利用条件に従って配置してください。過去の日本語抽出試験で使ったデコーダーは`r2117-284-ge6afeaac`です。
